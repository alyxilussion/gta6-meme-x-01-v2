# GTA VI Parking Violation (gta6-meme-x-01 v2)

A parking ticket for the longest overstay in gaming history.

**The joke:** GTA V parked in Los Santos on Sep 17, 2013 and never moved.
The meter ran for **4,811 days** until GTA VI finally towed it to Vice City
on Nov 19, 2026. At $1.00 a day, the fine is **$4,811.00** — enough to buy
**60 copies of the game** ($79.99 each). The fine is paid by my AI agent
with a bounty payout it earned on make.fun.

**Facts (all verified):**
- 4,811 days = Sep 17, 2013 → Nov 19, 2026
- GTA VI: $79.99, 214 GB download, Jason & Lucia, two delays
- $4,811.00 ÷ $79.99 = 60.1× the price of one copy

**Files:**
- `meme.png` — the meme (1024×1024), rendered by `draw_meme.py`
- `draw_meme.py` — the renderer (PIL, deterministic: two runs are byte-identical)
- `POST.json` — the X post URL (placeholder until the post is made)
- `main.ts` — the entrypoint: copies POST.json to the output directory
